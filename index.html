About The Project

“FOCUS – Learning to See Again” is a visually driven short film that follows Ella, a young photographer struggling with grief after losing her close friend and mentor, Liam. Once inspired by his encouragement and love for photography, Ella becomes emotionally disconnected from both her creativity and the world around her. When she discovers an old memory card filled with photographs and videos Liam captured of her, she begins to reconnect with the memories, places, and emotions they once shared. The film uses minimal dialogue and relies heavily on cinematography, colour grading, lighting, and sound design to communicate emotional progression. Through the gradual transition from muted, desaturated tones to warmer colours, the narrative symbolises Ella’s journey from isolation towards healing and rediscovery. Developed using DaVinci Resolve, Adobe After Effects, and a hybrid production methodology, “FOCUS” explores themes of loss, memory, personal growth, and the power of art as a form of emotional recovery.

Technologies used: DaVinvi Resolve, Adobe After Effects, StoryTribe, AutoDesk Flow Production Tracking.
