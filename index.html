About The Project

“FOCUS – Learning to See Again” is a visually driven short film that follows Ella, a young photographer struggling with grief after losing her close friend and mentor, Liam.

Once inspired by his encouragement and love for photography, Ella becomes emotionally disconnected.

She later discovers an old memory card filled with his photos of her, helping her reconnect with memory and emotion.

The film uses cinematography, colour grading, lighting, and sound design to communicate emotional progression.

Technologies used: DaVinci Resolve, Adobe After Effects, StoryTribe, Autodesk Flow Production Tracking.
